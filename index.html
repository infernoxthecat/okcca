The problem

Right now, finding out what a CCA is actually like takes a whole semester of guesswork.

Without ok!cca

  • → Six different Telegram/Instagram channels to track, half of them inactive
  • → Official descriptions that all read the same: "passionate", "family", "come join us!"
  • → The honest take only exists if you happen to know a member
  • → You commit to a CIP/CCA point requirement before you know if it's a fit

With ok!cca

  • → Every CCA in one place, posting real updates the way they'd post on Instagram
  • → "Day in the life" posts from actual members — the commitments, the fun, the fine print
  • → A 2-minute onboarding quiz that shortlists CCAs based on how you actually want to spend your time
  • → Know the vibe before the first meeting, not after three

Feature 01

Know the CCA before the first meeting.

Every club page brings together the details students actually need: upcoming events, time and commitment levels, member expectations, and honest on-the-ground reviews about what the club feels like in day-to-day life.

Feature 02

A 2-minute quiz that shortlists CCAs for you, on day one.

During onboarding, answer a handful of questions about how you like to spend your time and what you're looking for — community, competition, creative outlet, resume line. ok!cca matches you to CCAs worth exploring first, instead of scrolling through all of them cold.
